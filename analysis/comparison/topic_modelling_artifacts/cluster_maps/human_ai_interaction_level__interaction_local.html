The content follows Gustav, a giraffe obsessed with human language, who travels to Amsterdam and helps run a language school with Josephine and Leo, teaching Dutch and a wide array of other languages such as Danish, German, Finnish, Russian, Kurdish, and Mauritian Creole. It weaves together themes of linguistic diversity, the functional and cognitive dimensions of language, and the power of storytelling to connect people across cultures—while also exploring how words can both unite and influence communities.
